How to Play

1

Hold the LEFT micro:bit (shows L) in your left hand and the RIGHT micro:bit (shows R) in your right hand. The receiver stays plugged into the computer.

2

Stand in a boxing stance with space around you. Throw quick, snappy punches into the air — never at a real object. A short, sharp jab works better than a slow swing.

3

Shir walks by himself. When an electronic enemy blocks the road, the fight begins. Watch the enemy wind up (it pulls its arm back and trembles) — hitting it during the wind-up can interrupt the attack and deals counter damage.

4

Alternate left and right to build combos. Stronger punches do slightly more damage, but speed and rhythm matter more than force.

5

Defeat all five opponents — Resistor, Arduino Uno, micro:bit V2, Raspberry Pi and the ESP32 Overlord — to claim the throne.

⌨

Testing without hardware: J left punch, K right punch (Shift = strong), mouse left/right button. Esc pauses, F2 toggles the debug panel.
